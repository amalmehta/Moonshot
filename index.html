How this site works

Derive

Every model starts from conservation laws and is written out with symbols explained, plus what it leaves out. No black boxes.

Simulate

Ascent, landing, nozzle flow, orbits, constellations and the Earth–Moon trip are numerically integrated in your browser, not pre-recorded animations.

Check

Model outputs are compared with SpaceX, NASA, FAA and FCC figures on the Validation page, and run as automated tests.
